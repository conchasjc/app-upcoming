Getting in Sharing Photo booth Tips

Step one

1 Getting in

  1. Scan the QR code on your table or on the big screen, or open photovid.jcjecelwedding.website.
  2. If asked, enter the event code shown at the venue and tap Continue.
  3. Type your name and tap Start sharing, so we know who captured each moment. Prefer not to? Tap Stay anonymous.

You can change your name anytime: tap change next to Sharing as.

Step two

2 Sharing photos & videos

  1. Tap Choose photos & videos and pick as many as you like from your gallery.
  2. Or tap Take a photo to snap one with your camera right away.
  3. Want to say something? Write in Leave a message before choosing your photos.
  4. Your photos send in the background, each with its own progress bar and a ✓ when it's done.
  5. Everything you shared appears under Your memories, and may soon show up on the big screen.
📶

Weak signal? No worries: uploads pick up where they left off. If one fails, tap Retry failed.

Step three

3 The photo booth

Tap Photo booth on the upload page to open it. Allow camera access when your phone asks.

  1. Pick a layout at the top:
    • Single
    • Boomerang
    • Duo
    • Pair
    • Strip
    • Trio
    • Collage
    • Feature
    • Grid
    • Classic
    • Mosaic
    • Six
    Multi-photo layouts take several shots in a row. Boomerang makes a short looping clip, so move!
  2. Choose your look: Looks for camera styles (black & white, film, glow…), or Frames for our wedding frames.
  3. Set the self-timer and the screen flash for selfies in low light. Flip between front and back cameras anytime.
  4. Tap the big round button to take the photo. Strike a pose when the countdown starts!
  5. For layouts with several shots, tap Keep to go on, or Retake any shot.
  6. Add a message, then tap Share to send it to us, and Download to keep a copy.

On some phones an HD button appears: it opens your camera app for the sharpest single photo.

Good to know

  • 📱Keep it openStay on the page until your photos finish sending.
